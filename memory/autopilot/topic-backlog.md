# IdeoLab Blog Topic Backlog (seed clusters)

The autopilot picks the money page with the fewest published posts, then the
best-scoring unused keyword in that cluster (DataForSEO first, this table as
fallback). Keep slugs kebab-case containing the keyword. Every keyword idea is
tagged with the audience side it is written for (A = agency and growth
operators, B = business owners and in-house). Alternate sides day to day.

Keyword ideas come from the search-intent libraries in
`knowledge/marketing-source-of-truth.md` and the pain hierarchies in
`knowledge/icp-profile.md`. They are intent phrases, not claimed volumes.
