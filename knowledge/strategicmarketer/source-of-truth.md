# Strategic Marketer: Source of Truth

Read with `prompt/strategicmarketer-brand-and-icp.md`. That file is the brand, the
voice and the audience. This file is what the company offers, how it works, what it
can claim, and where the edges are.

**Where this comes from.** Every line below was read off strategicmarketer.com on
8 October 2026: the single page site at `/` and the page at `/for-agencies.html`.
Nothing here was supplied by David and nothing here was inferred. Where the site is
silent, this file says so rather than filling the gap.

**Why it was written.** David's notes of 29 September define a Source of Truth as

## 1. What the company is

Strategic Marketer helps established businesses identify, install and activate
practical AI systems built around their brand, their knowledge, their customers and
their growth priorities, not around the latest tool.

The headline: "Stop Experimenting With AI. Start Putting It to Work."
The strapline it repeats: "Less Experimenting. More Implementing."
Positioning line in the header: "AI Systems for Established Businesses".

Registered address on the site: PO Box 2792, Jupiter, FL 33468.

The argument the site is built on, in its own words: the problem is not access to
AI, it is knowing what to actually do with it. It calls this an implementation
problem rather than a knowledge problem. "Information keeps arriving. Systems never
get installed."

---

## 2. What it offers

There are three things the site sells, and only three.

**a. The AI Business Assessment.** The entry point, and the only action the site
asks a visitor to take. Described as "a working session about your business, not a
software demo" and "a focused, consultative conversation, not a sales pitch".

**b. Installed AI systems built on the Business Intelligence Agent.** This is the
delivery work. Section 4 lists what it covers.

**c. Licensing, for agencies.** Covered in section 7.

A "Free Training" also exists on the site, offered in the footer and in a popup
("Watch the Free Training"). The site gives no description of its contents. It is
recorded here because it exists, not because articles should point to it. The
approved action stays the assessment.

---

## 3. How the work happens

The assessment runs in five stages, in this order and with these names:

1. **Understand Your Growth.** Review the business, goals, current systems and how
   AI is being used today.
2. **Diagnose Growth Gaps.** Identify the gaps, bottlenecks and missing foundational
   intelligence holding AI back.
3. **Prioritize.** Determine where AI can create the greatest near term business
   value for that business specifically.
4. **Map.** Map the right combination of technology, implementation, automation,
   training or managed support.
5. **Activate Your Business Intelligence Agent.** A practical roadmap to move from
   experimentation to implementation.

What the site promises a visitor walks away with, whether or not they ever work
together, and this is the full list:

- A clear diagnosis of what is holding AI back in the business
- The two or three places AI would pay for itself first
- A prioritized roadmap they keep either way

It adds: "No pressure and no obligation. If we're not the right fit, we'll tell you,
and point you in a better direction." And: "One conversation. That is all it takes."

## 4. What gets installed: the Business Intelligence Agent

The site calls this "the foundation of what makes your business your business,
powering every AI system that follows", and states the principle plainly: AI has to
understand the business before it can work for the business.

**Seven foundation elements**, with the site's own one line descriptions:

| Element | What the site says it is |
|---|---|
| Brand Identity | Who you are: your name, look, values and the way you present the business |
| Brand Voice | How you sound, so every word reads like you wrote it, not like AI |
| Source of Truth | One approved set of facts about your business that every system relies on |
| Business Knowledge | What your company knows and how it actually works, captured once |
| Products and Services | What you sell, how it's priced, and how you describe it |
| Customer Intelligence | Who your customers are, what they ask for, and what they need |
| Competitive Positioning | Where you stand in your market and why customers choose you |

**Nine areas it then powers:** Content, Visibility, Marketing, Sales, Customer
Communication, Automation, Internal Knowledge, Operations, Team Productivity.

**Six named capabilities**, each stated on the site with the foundation elements it
draws on. The pairings matter and should not be swapped:

| Capability | Draws on |
|---|---|
| Consistent Brand Visibility | Brand Identity, Brand Voice |
| Better Content, Faster | Brand Voice, Business Knowledge |
| Stronger Customer Follow Up | Customer Intelligence, Brand Voice |
| Sales Enablement | Products and Services, Competitive Positioning |
| Automated Repetitive Work | Business Knowledge, Source of Truth |
| Centralized Business Knowledge | Source of Truth, Business Knowledge |

The site's reason for the structure: every capability draws from the same agent,
"which is why the results stay consistent instead of drifting apart".

**What the site says goes wrong without the foundation:** generic output that could
belong to anyone, inconsistent messaging from tool to tool, incorrect assumptions
stated confidently, off brand tone, disconnected work that never compounds, and low
value automation of the wrong things.

## 6. Differentiators

The site's five part answer to why them, with its own labels:

- **Build It.** Their own technology, not repackaged third party tools.
- **Fit It.** Shaped around how the business already runs.
- **Install It.** Activated inside the company, not left as advice.
- **Train Your Team.** The client's people learn to run it confidently.
- **Stay On It.** "We stick around until it's working every day."

The contrast it draws: "Most consultants hand you a list of AI tools and leave. We
do the opposite."

Six credential chips sit beside it: 15+ Years of Technology, Proprietary Technology,
Business Strategy First, Implementation Experience, Training and Coaching, Cross
Industry Depth.

The quote the page closes the section with: "Strategy without systems is a wish
list. Systems without strategy is expensive noise. You need both, working together."

## 7. The agency side

A separate page at `/for-agencies.html`. Agencies license Strategic Marketer
technology and deliver it under their own brand.

**Three things are licensable, named on the page:**

- **AI Marketing Systems.** "The same systems we install for our own clients."
- **Content Velocity Technology.** High volume, on brand content production without
  assembling the pipeline.
- **IdeoLab Technology.** "A complete AI platform behind your service offering:
  knowledge, creation and automation in one place."

This is the documented link between the two brands, and it is the reason David's
Content Velocity ICP sits in this folder. It does not change the rule in the brand
file: an article on strategicmarketer.com does not mention IdeoLab.

**How it works, three steps:** talk through the client base, license the solutions
that fit, deliver under the agency's own brand. The page promises no channel
conflict, that the agency stays the face of the relationship, and that the
technology, infrastructure and support stay Strategic Marketer's problem.

The action on this page is "Book a Conversation", not the assessment.

## 9. Limitations: what the site does not say

Every item below was looked for on 8 October 2026 and is absent. An article may not
state, imply or estimate any of them.

- **No price.** Not for the assessment, not for any system, not for licensing. There
  is no pricing page and no figure anywhere on either page.
- **No contract terms.** No length, no minimum, no renewal, no cancellation policy.
- **No timeline.** Nothing about how long an installation takes or when a client
  goes live.
- **No guarantee, refund or SLA.**
- **No integration list.** The site never names a CRM, a tool, a model or a vendor it
  connects to.
- **No team page or named staff** on either page fetched.
- **No geography stated.** A Florida address is given. Nothing says where clients
  can be.
- **No stated way to buy the Business Intelligence Agent on its own.** It is
  described as the foundation of the work, never as a product with its own price.
- **No case study.** The proof on the site is testimonials, not before and after
  measurement.
- **No headcount, revenue or funding figures** for Strategic Marketer itself.

The rule from David's notes applies with full force here: the system should never
invent company facts simply because information is missing.
