## There is no Source of Truth for this site

The file named `Content_Velocity_SOT.txt` is not a source of truth. Its own
opening line reads "External-facing ideal customer profile for marketing
partners", and its contents are the ICP in short form. Both files were checked
on 8 October 2026 and both are ICP documents.

David's notes of 29 September define a Source of Truth as "what the company
actually offers, how it works, benefits, capabilities, use cases,
differentiators, limitations, and approved claims". Nothing here carries that.

**What follows from that, and it matters:**

His notes allow a missing ICP or Brand Voice to be inferred, and then say "The
system should never invent company facts simply because information is
missing". So for this site, articles and topics may be built on who the buyer
is and what they struggle with, and may not make claims about what Content
Velocity does, how it works or what it includes. There is no document to check
such a claim against.

If product led articles are wanted on this site, a Source of Truth has to be
asked for. Until then the ICP is the ceiling.
