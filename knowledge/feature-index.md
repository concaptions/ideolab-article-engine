# IdeoLab feature index (quick lookup)

One paragraph per feature, taken from the Public definition in knowledge/marketing-source-of-truth.md. Use this to pick the right capability fast, then read the full feature section in the source of truth for use cases, FAQs and search intent before writing about it. Only describe capabilities listed here or in the source of truth.


## Discover & Remix Templates
Discover is the template gallery - “curated and community-built starting points.” Each entry is a ready-made lab (tagged LAB) you can use as a head start instead of building from an empty canvas. Entries range from official templates (e.g. Secret Webinar Builder Lab Template) to community-shared labs (e.g. COVID-19 Learning Lab, Helix Beta Initiative). Reached from the Discover item in the left sidebar (or View All in the Discover strip on the dashboard).

## Multi-Business / Agency Workspaces
The Businesses page lists every business you own. A business is the top-level container that holds labs, automations, and creations, and carries its own setup (company profile) and. Every account has at least one business (an Agency-type business by default). Reached from Businesses in the left sidebar.

## Labs: Connected AI Workspaces
A Lab is the core workspace in IdeoLab - a canvas where sources (URLs, docs, videos, social posts, research-leads) and AI-created outputs (images, slides, graphics, social posts, mindmaps) live side-by-side as cards. Each lab belongs to a Business and has chats, creations, and uploaded resources scoped to it.

## Canvas Design Elements
Opens the Design elements picker - a dropdown panel (not a modal) with a search bar and four categories of decorative, drag-and-droppable items that live on the canvas. These are decoration/annotation items - they are not chat-created; you place them by dragging them out of the panel onto the canvas, where each becomes a normal movable, resizable, deletable card.

## Upload Video & Analyze Recordings
Upload a video file from your computer. IdeoLab watches and catalogs what’s on screen and transcribes the audio, then lands the video on the canvas as a card so chats and workflows can answer questions about it.

## URL & Web Page Sources
Bring in a public web page, shared document, or spreadsheet as research material so IdeoLab can help summarize it, answer questions about it, or turn its content into new marketing work.

## Canvas Text Nodes
Adds an editable text card directly to the canvas. No modal opens - the card appears immediately, ready to type into. The card has a green header with a T icon and a three-dot menu, plus a small card toolbar (Full Screen · Zoom · Copy Text). It’s good for notes, briefs, labels, and reusable prompts you want kept on the canvas.

## Import Social Posts as Sources
Paste a public post URL from Facebook · X · Instagram · TikTok; IdeoLab imports the caption/text/details as a card. As the modal puts it: “Paste a public post from Facebook, X, Instagram or TikTok - we’ll import the caption/text and details.”

## Document & Spreadsheet Sources
Upload a document; IdeoLab pulls out text, headings, and tables so chats and workflows can quote it. Supported formats: TXT · PDF (text) · DOCX · CSV · XLSX · XLS. Max 100 MB / ~500 pages.

## YouTube Video & Channel Research
Bring YouTube content in as research material. The YouTube Import Studio imports a single video - or scans a creator’s channel and bulk-imports selected uploads - and fetches each video’s full title, transcript, and stats automatically so chats can reference what was said.

## Image Sources & Visual Understanding
Upload one or more image files from your computer. They land as image cards on the canvas, where IdeoLab reads any on-image text and tags key visuals. Images can be used as a reference for Edit Image or Blend Images, or as a source for analysis (e.g. caption creation, brand audit).

## Audio Recording & Transcription
Two ways to add audio to the lab as a transcribable card. Opens a dropdown with Record Audio (in-browser recording) and Upload Audio File (existing file).

## Canvas Grouping
An action (not a modal) - select multiple cards first, then click Group (layers/stack icon, 11th in the resource bar) to combine them into a single grouped card that moves and organizes together. Clicking Group with nothing selected does nothing.

## Multiple Chat Cards & Creation Hubs
The Chat item spawns a new chat card on the canvas - and that panel is the single most important object in a lab. Everything else in the resource bar brings material onto the canvas; the chat card is where that material gets turned into something.

## Prompt Templates Library
The Templates pill in the chat composer opens the Prompt Templates library - a full-screen view of ready-made prompt starters. The page has a Search… field, a + New Template button (to save your own), and a grid of template cards. Each card shows an icon, a title, a short description, and a category tag (e.g. Sales & Marketing) - for example “Sentiment Analysis and Marketing Strategies - turn your latest customer reviews into clear, growth-ready insights…”.

## Live Web Search in Chat
Enable web search when current public information is important to the task. IdeoLab can use live web information to support the response or creation.

## Per-Message Reference Images
The paperclip icon in the chat composer footer lets you attach an image from your computer as a reference for the next Send. The AI uses it as a visual cue (palette, composition, subject) alongside your prompt text.

## @-Mention Existing Visual Creations
Typing @ in a Graphics-mode chat composer (Flyers, Infographics, Thumbnails) opens a popup that lets you reference existing studio cards on this lab’s canvas as source images for the upcoming creation. The mentioned image becomes a visual reference the AI uses alongside your prompt.

## Persistent Connected Context
You can wire any canvas card (an image studio card, a text card, a URL card, a research card, etc.) into a chat card by dragging from its connection handle to the chat. From that point on, the connected card feeds into the AI as context for every Send in that chat - without you having to @-mention or re-attach it each time.

## Infinite Canvas Workflow
The infinite 2D board inside a lab where every card (card) lives. Sources, chats, created assets, design elements - all live as cards. Pannable, zoomable, multi-selectable.

## Website Deep Research
Analyses one website end-to-end. You provide a Website URL and choose how many Pages to Research (10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 75, 100, or 125), then click Start Website Research.

## Google Business / Local Business Research
Pulls a live Google Business (Google Business Profile / Maps) listing into the lab. Add a listing via either tab - Search Listing (type a business name, pick from results) or Paste Link (paste a Google Business URL) - and optionally toggle which data to pull in: GBL Details · SEO Optimization · Last 20 Reviews · Website Analysis. Click Research Businesses (the modal’s button; the card on the Choose-type screen reads Add Business Listing) and the listing appears as a canvas card.

## Find Local Businesses & Lead Research
Finds local lead opportunities by Industry / Keyword, City, and State / Region, with a Number Of Leads selector (e.g. 25). Clicking Find Local Businesses opens a fullscreen lead workspace where you review, filter, and download the leads - or add selected leads to the canvas as source cards for prospecting, lead qualification, and outreach planning.

## Text to Image
Text to Image creates a brand-new image from a written prompt - no source file needed. Describe what you want to see, choose creation options such as aspect ratio, and IdeoLab produces it in roughly 15-30 seconds. Every Send produces a new Image Studio holding one image, or several if you request multiple variations.

## Edit Image
Edit Image takes a source image you already have and transforms it based on a written instruction - removing elements, swapping backgrounds, changing the style, adjusting lighting, or adding new details - while keeping the rest of the image recognisable. Every Send produces a new Image Studio holding one edited result, or several if you request multiple variations.

## Blend Images
Blend Images combines two or more of your own images into a new visual. You can describe which subjects, backgrounds, styles, or visual elements should carry forward, then review and refine the resulting image in Image Studio.

## AI Photo Shoot
Photo Shoot produces a coordinated gallery of images centred on a single subject - people, a product, a service, or a location. Rather than creating one image from a prompt and being done, it runs a two-step creative process: first it builds a creative brief and 6 sample shots for you to review, then it produces the full gallery after you approve the direction or ask for a reshoot. The result is a set of images that share consistent style, lighting, and mood while varying in framing, angle, and moment - the output of a professional photo shoot, created in minutes.

## Image Studio
Image Studio is the full-screen editor that opens from any Image Studio - whether it was created by Text to Image, Edit Image, Blend Images, or Photo Shoot. It gives you two ways to work with the images after the creation is made: AI-prompted editing and upscaling - plus a gallery view for working across multiple images at once, and a Details panel showing provenance information for each image.

## Topic-to-Social Posts
Topic is the most open-ended Social Skill: describe any theme, idea, or message and the AI creates a batch of social media posts around it. No URL, photos, or video needed - just a description of what the posts should be about. Every Send produces a Social Studio holding the created posts, with per-platform previews for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and Twitter.

## Webpage-to-Social Posts
Page URL lets you paste any public webpage and create a batch of social media posts from its content. The AI reads the page - a blog post, a product page, a press release, a landing page - and turns it into platform-ready posts. Every Send produces a Social Studio holding the created posts, with per-platform previews for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and Twitter.

## Photos-to-Social Posts
Photos turns a batch of uploaded images into a set of social media posts - one post per photo. Upload your photos, describe the mood or theme, and the AI writes a caption and hashtags for each image. Every Send produces a Social Studio holding the created posts, with per-platform previews for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and Twitter.

## Video-to-Social Posts
Video turns a video you’ve added to your lab into a batch of social media posts. The AI analyses the video content - transcripts, scenes, key moments - and creates posts around it. Describe the angle you want, choose how many posts to create, and the AI writes captions and hashtags ready for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and Twitter. Every Send produces a Social Studio holding the created posts.

## Social Studio: Review, Edit, Publish & Schedule
Social Studio is the full-screen editor that opens from any created social post batch - created by Topic, Page URL, Photos, or Video. It gives you everything you need to review, edit, and publish your posts: per-platform previews (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter), a caption editor, an AI Pilot panel for post-level text and image edits, download options, and a publish flow.

## Social Analytics & AI Learning Loop
Social Analytics is a founder-confirmed IdeoLab capability designed to centralize social performance and let AI use what is working as input for future messaging and content decisions.

## AI Infographics
Infographics creates a structured, multi-section visual - data, comparisons, processes, timelines, anatomy diagrams. Every Send produces a new Studio with one infographic in it - or several, if you ask for multiple variations. You control the layout (Type), the look (Style), the orientation (Size), and how many you want at once (Variations).

## AI Flyers
Flyers creates a promotional 1-page graphic for events, sales, announcements, launches, parties, and the like. Each Send produces a new Studio with one flyer in it - or several, if you ask for multiple variations - assembled from a structured Creative Brief you review and approve first. Each flyer has a layered layout: headline, support copy, date and location, badge, CTA, hero / supporting / background imagery.

## AI Video Thumbnails
Thumbnails creates a click-driving image - sized and styled for YouTube, blog heroes, podcast artwork, course covers, and social cards. Every Send produces a Graphics Studio with the created thumbnail(s) in it.

## Graphics Studio
Graphics Studio is the full-screen editor that opens a Studio - the organizational group that holds the items produced by a Graphics creation (infographics, flyers, or thumbnails). Inside it you can refine any item, edit multiple items together via batch select, upscale, download, copy the original prompt, and (when launched from a lab) push edited variants back to the canvas.

## Concept Slides
Concept Slides is the AI-first slide-deck creator: you describe your topic, audience, and goal, and the AI structures and builds the complete presentation for you. Before any slide is rendered, the AI produces a per-slide Creative Brief in the chat - every slide shown as an editable block you can refine, reorder, add to, or regenerate before committing to full creation. Once you confirm the Creative Brief, the AI builds the finished visual deck slide by slide.

## Exact Slides
Exact Slides lets you write the slide-by-slide content yourself - titles, bullet points, body text, everything - then hands it to the AI to format and design into a finished slide deck. Where Concept Slides starts with a topic and walks through an AI-created Creative Brief before creating anything, Exact skips the Creative Brief step entirely and goes straight to creation. You own the content structure; the AI handles the visual presentation.

## Presentation Studio
Presentation Studio is the full-screen editor that opens from any created slide deck - whether it was created by Concept Slides or Exact Slides. It gives you three ways to work with slides after they’re created: direct text editing on any element, AI-powered element editing for targeted changes, and an AI chat panel for whole-deck revisions - plus speaker notes editing, slide deletion, version history, and export in four formats.

## AI Mindmaps
Mindmap turns any topic, information set, or question into a visual mind map - a branching diagram that organises ideas hierarchically and shows how concepts connect. Describe the subject you want mapped, and the AI structures it into a navigable mind map with labeled cards. Every Send produces a Mindmap Studio holding the created map, ready to edit, expand, and export.

## Mindmap Studio
Mindmap Studio is the full-screen editor that opens from any created mind map - created by the Mindmap Skill. It gives you full control over the map’s structure and content: add, delete, and rearrange cards; edit labels and colours; add notes to individual cards; and use the AI chat panel to make wholesale changes across the map. Export as PNG or PDF when it’s ready to share.

## Free Offer Lead Sites
Free Offer is a single-page lead-magnet site: it offers something of value - a downloadable guide, a checklist, a discount code, early access, or a free tool - in exchange for an email or contact details. It’s the flagship lead-capture page type in the AI Sites Assistant, and the AI builds a focused page whose single goal is the opt-in. Every section the AI lays down is in service of one conversion: getting the visitor to hand over their email to claim the offer.

## Event Registration Sites
Event is a single-page event registration site - a focused page whose one job is to turn interest in an event into a confirmed sign-up. You describe the event in plain language and the AI builds a page tuned to drive RSVPs: a webinar, a product launch, a conference, a workshop, a fundraiser, or a local meetup.

## Booking Sites
Booking is a single-page booking / appointment site - it presents one service clearly and captures a booking request or appointment enquiry through a form. Where a Free Offer trades something for an email and an Event drives sign-ups to a dated happening, a Booking page has one job: turn an interested visitor into a request for someone’s time. The whole page is built around a single, unmistakable “Book” or “Request appointment” call to action.

## Brand Sites
Brand Site is a general single-page brand or business landing page - who you are, what you offer, and a clear call to action, all on one scrollable page. It’s the broadest of the four Sites page types: where Free Offer, Event, and Booking each drive one specific action (opt in, register, book), a Brand Site exists to establish a presence. Pick it when the page is about being known rather than capturing a single conversion - when none of the other three types fit.

## Site Studio
Site Studio is the full-screen editor that opens from any created site - whether it was built directly or from a Vision Board. It’s where you refine the AI’s first draft into a finished, publishable site. You can work three ways - chat with the AI, click an element and edit it visually, or edit text inline - and the same studio handles publishing, lead capture, analytics, and SEO/domain settings.

## Workflow Automation Studio
Workflow Automation Studio helps a business turn recurring work into reusable routines. Instead of rebuilding the same content, reporting, follow-up, or coordination process every time, the team can create a repeatable automation, review it, and rely on it when that work needs to happen again.

## Automated IdeoLab Content Creation
IdeoLab Automations can help create marketing assets as part of recurring business routines. This makes it possible for ongoing content needs - such as social posts, graphics, presentations, images, or planning assets - to be produced more consistently without rebuilding the same task by hand each time.

## Connected Business Tools
Connected Business Tools help IdeoLab work alongside the software a business already uses for communication, email, spreadsheets, customer management, scheduling, forms, commerce, storage, and project coordination. The marketing value is less copy-paste work and fewer manual handoffs between systems.

## Automation Activity History
Automation Activity History gives teams a clear way to confirm that recurring work happened, see when something needs attention, and build confidence in the routines they rely on.

## Creative Brief Review Step
The Creative Brief is a review checkpoint used in selected creation workflows. It gives the user a chance to confirm or adjust direction before the finished asset is created.

## Studios: Persistent Refinement Workspaces
A Studio is the organizational unit that groups together related items produced by a creation. Every time you create something with an Assistant that supports the Studio pattern, the output isn’t a loose item - it’s a new Studio that contains one or more items (one for each variation you asked for in your prompt). Edits and upscales you make inside the Studio editor later are also added to the same Studio, so over time a Studio can grow.

## Creation: Finished AI Outputs
A creation is any asset a user makes with an Assistant - an image, a graphic (infographic / flyer / thumbnail), a set of social posts, a slide deck, or a mind map. Creations are the product’s core output, and they’re collected per business under My Creations (Images · Graphics · Social · Slides · Mindmaps).

## Canvas Cards / Nodes
A canvas card is a single item that sits on a lab’s canvas. Everything that lives on the canvas is a card - whether it was brought in as source material or created by an Assistant. Cards are the building blocks of a lab: you arrange them spatially, wire them into chats, group them, and edit them.
